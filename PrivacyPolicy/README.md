# Privacy

SkyrimSE-DualScreen has no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no accounts of its own.

- **Steam:** you sign in to Steam directly, the same way the Steam app does. The app stores Steam's sign-in token on your device so it can sign in again. Your password is sent to Steam and never stored.
- **Nexus Mods:** your Nexus API key is picked up when you sign in on Nexus's own site and is stored on your device. It's only sent to Nexus Mods, to browse and download mods.
- **Updates:** the app asks GitHub for this project's latest release.
- **Your game:** your Skyrim folder, saves and mods stay on your device.

Nothing else leaves your device.
